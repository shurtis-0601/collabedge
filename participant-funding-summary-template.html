Forecast total = spend to date + future scheduled spend. Remaining = budget minus forecast total. A negative remaining figure means the current roster is projected to exceed the budget before plan end.

How status is set: status compares forecast spend against plan time elapsed. Therapy-type categories use a tighter margin than core supports, since a therapy waitlist is harder to recover from than a core overspend. Mark a category as therapy using the checkbox in that row to apply the tighter threshold.
On Track Forecast spend is in line with plan time elapsed.
Watch Forecast spend is running ahead of plan time elapsed.
Over Budget Forecast total exceeds the remaining budget.
